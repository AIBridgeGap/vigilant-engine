# GoDaddy Website Builder: Demo Gallery Page Instructions

This guide will walk you through creating your AIBridgeGap.com Demo Gallery Page in the GoDaddy Website Builder. This page will showcase your interactive AI automation tools.

## Step 1: Create a New Page
1.  Log in to your GoDaddy account and navigate to your Website Builder dashboard.
2.  Click on **"Edit Site"** to open the website editor.
3.  In the left-hand menu, click on **"Pages"**.
4.  Click the **"+" (Add Page)** button.
5.  Choose **"New Page"** (or a similar option for a blank page).
6.  Name the page **"AI Demos"** or **"Demo Gallery"**.
7.  Click **"Create Page"**.

## Step 2: Add Sections to Your Demo Gallery Page

### Section 1: Demo Gallery Introduction
Provide an overview of what visitors can expect from your interactive demos.
1.  Click **"Add Section"** on your Demo Gallery page.
2.  Look for sections under **"Introduction"** or **"About"**. Choose a layout with a main heading and a paragraph or two.

### Section 2: Individual Demo Showcases
For each AI demo, you will add a dedicated section. GoDaddy offers various section types that can accommodate embedded content or links.

**For each demo (No-Code AI Workflow, AI Meeting Assistant, Email Response Generator, AI Project Prioritizer):**
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Content"**, **"Features"**, **"Gallery"**, or **"HTML"** (if you plan to embed directly).
3.  Choose a layout that allows for:
    *   A clear title for the demo.
    *   A brief description of what the demo does and its business value.
    *   A prominent area for the interactive demo itself (e.g., an embedded iFrame, a link to a separate demo page, or a video walkthrough).
    *   A call-to-action (e.g., "Try the Demo," "Learn More").

    *Self-Correction:* Since GoDaddy's HTML section has limitations, for complex interactive demos, it's often best to host the demo on a separate platform (like a simple web server or a specialized demo hosting service) and then embed it using an iFrame or link to it. For simpler demos, the HTML section might suffice.

### Section 3: Call-to-Action (Final)
Encourage visitors to connect or learn more after experiencing the demos.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Call to Action"** or **"Contact"**. Choose a prominent layout with a clear message and a button.
3.  Customize the text and link the button to your Contact Page or Services Page.

## Step 3: Customize Content and Design
1.  **Replace Placeholder Text**: Copy and paste the content provided in the `demo_gallery_page_content.md` file into the respective sections.
2.  **Integrate Demos**: This is the most technical part. For each demo section, you will either:
    *   **Embed an iFrame**: If your demo is hosted externally, use GoDaddy's HTML section to embed an iFrame pointing to your demo's URL.
    *   **Link to External Demo**: Create a button or link that takes users to a separate page where the demo is hosted.
    *   **Video Walkthrough**: Embed a video (e.g., from YouTube or Vimeo) demonstrating the tool.
3.  **Upload Images**: Use screenshots or conceptual images for each demo.
4.  **Adjust Styling**: Maintain consistent fonts, colors, and overall layout with your other pages.
5.  **Review Responsiveness**: Check how your page looks on different devices.

## Step 4: Publish Your Changes
1.  Once you are satisfied with your Demo Gallery Page, click the **"Publish"** button in the top right corner of the GoDaddy Website Builder to make your changes live.
