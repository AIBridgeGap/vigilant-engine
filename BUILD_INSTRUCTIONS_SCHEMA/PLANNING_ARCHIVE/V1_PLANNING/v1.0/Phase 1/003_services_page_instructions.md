# GoDaddy Website Builder: Services Page Instructions

This guide will walk you through creating your AIBridgeGap.com Services Page in the GoDaddy Website Builder. This page will detail your core offerings.

## Step 1: Create a New Page
1.  Log in to your GoDaddy account and navigate to your Website Builder dashboard.
2.  Click on **"Edit Site"** to open the website editor.
3.  In the left-hand menu, click on **"Pages"**.
4.  Click the **"+" (Add Page)** button.
5.  Choose **"New Page"** (or a similar option for a blank page).
6.  Name the page **"Services"**.
7.  Click **"Create Page"**.

## Step 2: Add Sections to Your Services Page

### Section 1: Services Introduction
Provide an overview of your service philosophy.
1.  Click **"Add Section"** on your Services page.
2.  Look for sections under **"Introduction"** or **"About"**. Choose a layout with a main heading and a paragraph or two.

### Section 2: Detailed Service Offerings
Detail each of your core services.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Content"**, **"Features"**, or **"Services"**. Choose a layout that allows for multiple distinct blocks, each with a title, a detailed description, and potentially an icon or image.
3.  You will add a block for each of your main services: Process Automation, AI Prompt Engineering, Internal AI Training, Workflow Optimization, and Custom AI Integration.

### Section 3: How We Work / Our Approach
Explain your methodology or client engagement process.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Process"**, **"How It Works"**, or **"About"**. A step-by-step or multi-column layout would be suitable.

### Section 4: Call-to-Action (Final)
Encourage visitors to take the next step.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Call to Action"** or **"Contact"**. Choose a prominent layout with a clear message and a button.
3.  Customize the text and link the button to your Contact Page or a scheduling tool.

## Step 3: Customize Content and Design
1.  **Replace Placeholder Text**: Copy and paste the content provided in the `services_page_content.md` file into the respective sections.
2.  **Upload Images**: Use relevant icons or images for each service. Ensure images are optimized for web.
3.  **Adjust Styling**: Maintain consistent fonts, colors, and overall layout with your Home Page.
4.  **Review Responsiveness**: Check how your page looks on different devices.

## Step 4: Publish Your Changes
1.  Once you are satisfied with your Services Page, click the **"Publish"** button in the top right corner of the GoDaddy Website Builder to make your changes live.
