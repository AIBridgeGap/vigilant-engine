# GoDaddy Website Builder: Home Page Instructions

This guide will walk you through creating your AIBridgeGap.com Home Page in the GoDaddy Website Builder. Follow these steps to set up the page structure and add content.

## Step 1: Create a New Page
1.  Log in to your GoDaddy account and navigate to your Website Builder dashboard.
2.  Click on **"Edit Site"** to open the website editor.
3.  In the left-hand menu, click on **"Pages"** (usually represented by a page icon).
4.  Click the **"+" (Add Page)** button.
5.  Choose **"New Page"** (or a similar option for a blank page).
6.  Name the page **"Home"** and set it as your homepage if prompted.
7.  Click **"Create Page"**.

## Step 2: Add Sections to Your Home Page
GoDaddy Website Builder uses a section-based approach. You will add different types of sections to build out your page.

### Section 1: Hero Section (Introduction)
This is the first thing visitors see. It should clearly state what AIBridgeGap does.
1.  Click **"Add Section"** on your Home page.
2.  Look for sections under **"Introduction"**, **"Header"**, or **"Hero"**. Choose a layout that allows for a large headline, a subheading, and a prominent call-to-action button.
3.  Once added, you will customize the text and button.

### Section 2: Services Overview
Briefly introduce your core services.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Content"**, **"Features"**, or **"Services"**. Choose a layout that allows for multiple columns or cards, each with a title, a short description, and ideally an icon or image.
3.  Customize the content for 3-4 key services.

### Section 3: Value Proposition / Why Choose Us
Explain what makes AIBridgeGap unique.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"About"**, **"Features"**, or **"Why Choose Us"**. A layout with a clear heading and a few bullet points or short paragraphs would work well.
3.  Customize the content to highlight your unique approach (no-code, bridging the gap).

### Section 4: Featured Case Studies (Placeholder)
Showcase your success stories. You can add actual case studies later.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Case Studies"**, **"Portfolio"**, or **"Projects"**. A layout that allows for a title, brief description, and an image or link to a full case study is ideal.
3.  For now, you can use placeholder text and images.

### Section 5: Testimonials (Placeholder)
Build trust with client feedback. You can add actual testimonials later.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Testimonials"** or **"Reviews"**. Choose a layout that displays quotes and client names/titles.
3.  For now, use placeholder testimonials.

### Section 6: Call-to-Action (Final)
Encourage visitors to take the next step.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Call to Action"** or **"Contact"**. Choose a prominent layout with a clear message and a button.
3.  Customize the text and link the button to your Contact Page or a scheduling tool.

## Step 3: Customize Content and Design
1.  **Replace Placeholder Text**: Copy and paste the content provided in the `home_page_content.md` file into the respective sections.
2.  **Upload Images**: Replace placeholder images with your logo, relevant stock photos, or images representing your services. Ensure images are optimized for web (e.g., compressed).
3.  **Adjust Styling**: Use GoDaddy's design tools to set fonts, colors, and overall layout to match your brand (e.g., AIBridgeGap's blue/teal palette).
4.  **Review Responsiveness**: Check how your page looks on different devices (desktop, tablet, mobile) using the preview options in the builder.

## Step 4: Publish Your Changes
1.  Once you are satisfied with your Home Page, click the **"Publish"** button in the top right corner of the GoDaddy Website Builder to make your changes live.
