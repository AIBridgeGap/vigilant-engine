# AIBridgeGap.com Website Build Order Prioritization

This document outlines the recommended order for building your AIBridgeGap.com website pages in GoDaddy, prioritized by criticality and importance for a consulting business.

## Phase 1: Most Critical (Foundation & Core Business)
These pages are essential for establishing your online presence and allowing potential clients to understand your offerings and contact you. Focus on completing these first.

1.  **Home Page**
    *   Instructions: `home_page_instructions.md`
    *   Content: `home_page_content.md`
    *   *Reasoning:* Your digital storefront, providing the first impression and a high-level overview of your value.

2.  **Services Page**
    *   Instructions: `services_page_instructions.md`
    *   Content: `services_page_content.md`
    *   *Reasoning:* Clearly outlines what you offer, crucial for client understanding and decision-making.

3.  **Contact Page**
    *   Instructions: `contact_page_instructions.md`
    *   Content: `contact_page_content.md`
    *   *Reasoning:* Absolutely vital for lead generation and allowing interested parties to reach out to you.

## Phase 2: Most Important (Credibility & Engagement)
These pages build trust, showcase your expertise, and provide deeper insights into your work. Build these after Phase 1 is complete.

4.  **About Page**
    *   Instructions: `about_page_instructions.md`
    *   Content: `about_page_content.md`
    *   *Reasoning:* Helps build rapport and credibility by sharing your story, vision, and expertise.

5.  **AI Demos / Demo Gallery Page**
    *   Instructions: `demo_gallery_page_instructions.md`
    *   Content: `demo_gallery_page_content.md`
    *   Implementation Guidance: `ai_demo_implementation_guidance.md`
    *   *Reasoning:* A key differentiator for AIBridgeGap, providing tangible proof of your capabilities and practical application of AI. While the implementation of the demos themselves might be complex, setting up the page structure for them is important early on.

## Phase 3: Growth & Resources (Long-Term Value)
These pages support long-term growth, thought leadership, and provide additional value, but are not critical for the initial launch. You can work on these after the core site is live.

6.  **Blog & Resources Page**
    *   Instructions: `blog_resources_page_instructions.md`
    *   Content: `blog_resources_page_content.md`
    *   *Reasoning:* Important for SEO, thought leadership, and providing valuable content, but can be built out with more articles over time after the core site is live.

By following this order, you will establish a strong, functional, and compelling online presence for AIBridgeGap.com efficiently.
