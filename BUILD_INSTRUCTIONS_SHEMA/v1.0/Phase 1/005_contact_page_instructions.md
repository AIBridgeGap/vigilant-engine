# GoDaddy Website Builder: Contact Page Instructions

This guide will walk you through creating your AIBridgeGap.com Contact Page in the GoDaddy Website Builder. This page will provide visitors with various ways to get in touch.

## Step 1: Create a New Page
1.  Log in to your GoDaddy account and navigate to your Website Builder dashboard.
2.  Click on **"Edit Site"** to open the website editor.
3.  In the left-hand menu, click on **"Pages"**.
4.  Click the **"+" (Add Page)** button.
5.  Choose **"New Page"** (or a similar option for a blank page).
6.  Name the page **"Contact"**.
7.  Click **"Create Page"**.

## Step 2: Add Sections to Your Contact Page

### Section 1: Contact Introduction
Provide a welcoming message and explain how visitors can reach you.
1.  Click **"Add Section"** on your Contact page.
2.  Look for sections under **"Introduction"** or **"Contact"**. Choose a layout with a main heading and a brief introductory paragraph.

### Section 2: Contact Form
Allow visitors to send you messages directly through the website.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Contact"** or **"Form"**. Choose a standard contact form layout. GoDaddy forms usually allow you to customize fields and specify where submissions are sent (e.g., your email address).

### Section 3: Direct Contact Options & Scheduling
Provide alternative contact methods and a way to book appointments.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Contact"**, **"Text"**, or **"Embed"**. You can use a text section to list your email and phone number.
3.  For scheduling, if GoDaddy has a direct integration for Calendly or similar, use that. Otherwise, you might need to use an "Embed" section to paste the Calendly embed code, or simply provide a direct link to your Calendly page.

### Section 4: Location (Optional)
If you have a physical office or prefer to show a general service area.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Map"** or **"Location"**. Add your address and a map if desired.

## Step 3: Customize Content and Design
1.  **Replace Placeholder Text**: Copy and paste the content provided in the `contact_page_content.md` file into the respective sections.
2.  **Configure Form**: Ensure your contact form is correctly set up to send submissions to your preferred email address.
3.  **Integrate Scheduling**: Add your Calendly link or embed code.
4.  **Adjust Styling**: Maintain consistent fonts, colors, and overall layout with your other pages.
5.  **Review Responsiveness**: Check how your page looks on different devices.

## Step 4: Publish Your Changes
1.  Once you are satisfied with your Contact Page, click the **"Publish"** button in the top right corner of the GoDaddy Website Builder to make your changes live.
