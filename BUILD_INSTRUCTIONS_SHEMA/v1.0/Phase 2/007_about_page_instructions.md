# GoDaddy Website Builder: About Page Instructions

This guide will walk you through creating your AIBridgeGap.com About Page in the GoDaddy Website Builder. This page will tell your company's story and highlight your expertise.

## Step 1: Create a New Page
1.  Log in to your GoDaddy account and navigate to your Website Builder dashboard.
2.  Click on **"Edit Site"** to open the website editor.
3.  In the left-hand menu, click on **"Pages"**.
4.  Click the **"+" (Add Page)** button.
5.  Choose **"New Page"** (or a similar option for a blank page).
6.  Name the page **"About"**.
7.  Click **"Create Page"**.

## Step 2: Add Sections to Your About Page

### Section 1: About Introduction / Company Story
Share the founding story and mission of AIBridgeGap.
1.  Click **"Add Section"** on your About page.
2.  Look for sections under **"About"** or **"Introduction"**. Choose a layout with a main heading, a compelling image (e.g., a professional headshot or a conceptual image representing AIBridgeGap), and a few paragraphs.

### Section 2: Your Profile / Expertise Highlights
Detail your background, expertise, and qualifications.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Team"**, **"About"**, or **"Skills"**. A layout that allows for a photo, title, and bullet points or short paragraphs detailing your key skills and experience would be ideal.

### Section 3: Vision & Values
Communicate what drives AIBridgeGap and its core principles.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Values"**, **"Mission"**, or **"About"**. A layout with clear headings for each value and a brief explanation is recommended.

### Section 4: Partners / Tools (Optional)
Showcase the platforms and technologies you work with.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Partners"**, **"Logos"**, or **"Tools"**. A section that displays logos of the platforms you use (e.g., Make.com, Zapier, Google Workspace) can add credibility.

### Section 5: Call-to-Action (Final)
Encourage visitors to connect or learn more.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Call to Action"** or **"Contact"**. Choose a prominent layout with a clear message and a button.
3.  Customize the text and link the button to your Contact Page or Services Page.

## Step 3: Customize Content and Design
1.  **Replace Placeholder Text**: Copy and paste the content provided in the `about_page_content.md` file into the respective sections.
2.  **Upload Images**: Use a professional headshot for your profile, and relevant conceptual images or tool logos. Ensure images are optimized for web.
3.  **Adjust Styling**: Maintain consistent fonts, colors, and overall layout with your other pages.
4.  **Review Responsiveness**: Check how your page looks on different devices.

## Step 4: Publish Your Changes
1.  Once you are satisfied with your About Page, click the **"Publish"** button in the top right corner of the GoDaddy Website Builder to make your changes live.
