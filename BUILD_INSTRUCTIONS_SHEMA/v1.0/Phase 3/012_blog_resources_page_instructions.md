# GoDaddy Website Builder: Blog/Resources Page Instructions

This guide will walk you through creating your AIBridgeGap.com Blog/Resources Page in the GoDaddy Website Builder. This page will serve as a hub for educational content and insights.

## Step 1: Create a New Page
1.  Log in to your GoDaddy account and navigate to your Website Builder dashboard.
2.  Click on **"Edit Site"** to open the website editor.
3.  In the left-hand menu, click on **"Pages"**.
4.  Click the **"+" (Add Page)** button.
5.  Choose **"New Page"** (or a similar option for a blank page).
6.  Name the page **"Blog & Resources"**.
7.  Click **"Create Page"**.

## Step 2: Add Sections to Your Blog/Resources Page

### Section 1: Blog Introduction
Provide an overview of what visitors can expect from your blog.
1.  Click **"Add Section"** on your Blog/Resources page.
2.  Look for sections under **"Introduction"** or **"About"**. Choose a layout with a main heading and a paragraph or two.

### Section 2: Blog Posts / Articles
This is where your articles will be displayed. GoDaddy's builder usually has a dedicated blog section.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Blog"** or **"Posts"**. Choose a layout that displays blog post summaries (title, excerpt, image, read more button).
3.  You will need to add individual blog posts through GoDaddy's blog management system, not directly on this page. This section will dynamically display them.

### Section 3: Newsletter Signup
Encourage visitors to subscribe for updates.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Subscribe"**, **"Newsletter"**, or **"Contact"**. Choose a layout with a clear call-to-action and an email input field.

### Section 4: Call-to-Action (Final)
Encourage visitors to connect or learn more.
1.  Click **"Add Section"**.
2.  Look for sections under **"Call to Action"** or **"Contact"**. Choose a prominent layout with a clear message and a button.
3.  Customize the text and link the button to your Contact Page or Services Page.

## Step 3: Customize Content and Design
1.  **Replace Placeholder Text**: Copy and paste the content provided in the `blog_resources_page_content.md` file into the respective sections.
2.  **Add Blog Posts**: Go to GoDaddy's blog management area (usually accessible from the main dashboard or a specific blog tab within the editor) to create your first few blog posts. This section will then populate automatically.
3.  **Adjust Styling**: Maintain consistent fonts, colors, and overall layout with your other pages.
4.  **Review Responsiveness**: Check how your page looks on different devices.

## Step 4: Publish Your Changes
1.  Once you are satisfied with your Blog/Resources Page, click the **"Publish"** button in the top right corner of the GoDaddy Website Builder to make your changes live.
